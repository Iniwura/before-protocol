## Goal

Prove the actual released website can use **three distinct testnet wallet addresses** to complete:
**sponsor publishes a BTEST reward → researcher commits a finding → appointed reviewer approves that exact finding → researcher reveals after the deadline → escrow pays the BTEST reward and refunds the researcher's BTEST bond.**

These are functional acceptance tests with **valueless mock tokens**, not security audits or claims of independently discovered vulnerabilities. If one human controls the three wallets, label them **distinct test accounts under one operator**, not independent human reviewers.

## Prerequisites (never upload or share private keys)

- Use an EVM browser wallet on a desktop with **three separate addresses/accounts**:
  - **S:** sponsor, who funds and publishes the challenge.
  - **R:** researcher, who commits the finding and receives the payout.
  - **V:** appointed reviewer, who approves the report.
- S, R and V must have **some free testnet ETH** for their respective gas costs. The official faucet is linked above; it may require human verification. Testnet ETH and freely mintable BTEST have no monetary value.
- Do not use a wallet holding real assets for this rehearsal. Do not send real WIF to the escrow or share keystore/seed/password material with ChatGPT or GitHub.
- Use the *same browser* for researcher commit and reveal, or make a private offline backup of the **recovery key** generated by the app. Never include the key in screenshots, GitHub evidence, or messages.

## 1. Sponsor (S): publish one small test bounty

Open https://before-web-theta.vercel.app/app/bounty/ and choose **Post a test reward**.

1. Click **Connect wallet**; confirm Robinhood **Testnet 46630** and sponsor address S.
2. Paste reviewer address V (not S) into **Who will review the findings?**
3. Set **Reward for a valid finding** to `1` (BTEST).
4. Open **Change bond, deadlines or reviewer approvals**, choose:
   - **Reviewer approvals:** `1`
   - **Researcher deposit:** `0.1` BTEST
   - **Submission deadline:** `2` minutes
   - **Final reveal deadline:** `20` minutes
5. Click **Publish BTEST bounty**. Approve each **testnet** transaction separately. The app may deploy a new reviewer gate, mint BTEST, approve the exact mock reward and publish a challenge. All mined transactions must show success on Blockscout.
6. The website should display **Bounty #N is live**, without switching the sponsor into the researcher's workflow.
7. Copy the **researcher** and **reviewer** links. Record N and the **challenge creation transaction** URL. Do not assume N=5 because other users can publish challenges.

A short submission window reduces test time, but gives only 18 minutes after the two-minute commitment period to reveal. If users cannot switch wallets quickly, choose `5` minutes submission and `30` minutes reveal instead. All timing is enforced by real block timestamps.

## 2. Researcher (R): commit the report

Open the **researcher** invitation link from S.

1. Choose **Submit a finding** if prompted. Connect researcher address R (different from S and V).
2. Load Bounty #N from the link. Verify **1 BTEST reward**, **0.1 BTEST bond**, correct windows and reviewer quorum.
3. Use the exact report text below as synthetic **test evidence**, not a claim about a production vulnerability:

   `The BEFORE training vault restricts each release to 60 BTEST, but does not enforce a cumulative daily spending limit. Two otherwise allowed releases of 60 and 50 BTEST total 110 BTEST. This demonstrates why a per-transfer cap is not a daily cap. Practice/test asset only.`

4. Select **Generate key**; securely save the local recovery key, then **Commit finding**. The app uses a private hash commitment and a valueless BTEST bond. It may mint and approve BTEST before committing.
5. Confirm **Finding submitted** and the **Copy message for reviewer** option. Save the commitment transaction hash.
6. Send the review request text privately to V. BEFORE does **not** deliver messages for you; it only prepares the copyable message.

## 3. Reviewer (V): approve the actual evidence

Open the **reviewer** invitation link from S.

1. Choose **Review a finding** if prompted. Connect reviewer address V.
2. Verify Bounty #N. Read the exact report from R. Check the training-vault example against the source.
3. Enter R's exact address as **Solver wallet** and paste the exact report text as **Exact finding evidence**. Every space/character matters because the review binds its Keccak-256 hash.
4. Click **Record reviewer approval** and confirm the testnet wallet transaction.
5. The site should show **1 of 1 approvals recorded**. Save the reviewer approval transaction hash and tell R the report was approved.

This verifies the onchain quorum mechanism; if V is controlled by the same person as S or R, **do not call this independent review**.

## 4. Researcher (R): reveal and settle

After the real **submission deadline** has passed but **before the final reveal deadline**:

1. Reconnect address R. Open the same Bounty #N.
2. Reenter the original report text **exactly** and restore your recovery key from the same device or private backup.
3. Click **Claim approved reward**. The UI checks the exact committed digest and reviewer approval quorum before requesting an onchain transaction.
4. Confirm the transaction. Check the public escrow event **RewardPaid** and commitment status **Settled**.
5. Verify R receives **1.1 BTEST** from the escrow in total: **1 BTEST reward + original 0.1 BTEST bond**. Confirm **Bounty #N Closed** and the transaction URL.

No real WIF should be transferred at any point. Record only public wallet addresses, the bounty number, four public transaction hashes and Explorer links; exclude private recovery keys.
